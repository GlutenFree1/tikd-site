Tikd

Track who owes what, on your phone.

Tikd is an IOU tracker for iOS and Android. Record money you lend and borrow, log payments, set reminders and share statements. Your ledger stays on your device.

Support

Questions, bug reports or purchase problems: email justin@talisent.nz. Please include your phone model and the app version from Settings → About.

  • Restoring a purchase: open the upgrade screen and tap "Restore purchases", signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with.
  • Cancelling Tikd Monthly: manage subscriptions in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
  • Moving to a new phone: make a backup in Tikd, move the file to the new phone, then restore it in Tikd.
